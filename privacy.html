Privacy Policy

Last Vertex: Idle Defense · effective 12 October 2026

Last Vertex: Idle Defense ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This page explains what data the game handles. In short: the game does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, tattoos, settings, quest and achievement state) is saved only on your device, in the browser storage of the app. It is never uploaded to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. You can copy it yourself with Settings → Export Save.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The game has no user accounts and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. It does not use cookies, advertising IDs, location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

Network requests

The game is loaded from the developer's website (hosted on GitHub Pages). To keep in-game timers fair, it reads the current time from that website when you open it. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) may record technical request data such as your IP address in its server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not have access to these logs.

Bug reports and feedback

The bug button opens a Google Form in your browser. Sending it is optional. The form includes basic technical information about your game (game version, phone model, browser version, screen size and your graphics setting and progress) to help find bugs, plus anything you choose to write. Do not include personal information you don't want to share. Forms are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer only uses the answers to fix bugs and improve the game.

Gift codes

Gift codes are checked entirely on your device; nothing is sent when you redeem one.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if online features are added), this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions: use the bug button in the game, or open an issue at github.com/ahmetozeladam/last-vertex.

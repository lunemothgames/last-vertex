# Google Play store listing — Last Vertex: Idle Defense

Copy each block into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.
English is the default listing; add the others under **Manage translations → Add your own translations**
(Türkçe `tr-TR`, Español `es-419` and `es-ES`, Português `pt-BR` and `pt-PT`). Each language gets its own screenshots
from `screenshots/<lang>/`. Character limits are checked by `node tools/check-listing.js`.

Rules that keep a listing out of trouble: no "best", "#1", "free" in the title, no prices or promotions in the
text, no other games' names, no keyword lists. Keywords belong in natural sentences, as below.

### Full description (max 4000)
```
Idle tower defense on a retro CRT terminal: you are the last vertex, one glowing triangle at the bottom of the screen, and everything above it wants through.

Your triangle aims and fires on its own; you decide how it grows. Play actively or put the phone down and let it run.

▸ BUILD IT YOUR WAY
Pour coins into permanent upgrades: damage, fire rate, crits, multishot, ricochet, pierce, armor, thorns, regen, dodge, slow, knockback and more. A wall of thorns and armor, a crit cannon, a swarm of bouncing bullets — every build is viable.

▸ TATTOOS
Summon 28 tattoos across four rarities and fuse duplicates up to level 7. Passive tattoos shape your build; active ones add a button to the battle: freeze the field, blast every enemy, overdrive your fire rate or turn kills into gold; others shatter frozen enemies into chain reactions. The odds are shown on screen and an epic is guaranteed within 50 summons.

▸ ENDLESS WAVES, 30 TIERS
Fight through 30 tiers of rising danger: runners, tanks, splitters, shielded wardens, healing menders, teleporting blinkers and enraged bosses that call in reinforcements. Earn mastery stars in every tier for permanent bonuses.

▸ WEEKLY OP
Every week brings two new rule-changing mutators — horde, glass cannon, boss rush, titans and more — with gem rewards for the waves you reach.

▸ PLAY ACTIVE OR IDLE
Choose your target priority, speed up the game, time your abilities — or walk away. Idle income keeps earning while you're away, quests refresh through the day and achievements reward every milestone.

▸ RETRO TERMINAL LOOK
Scanlines, phosphor glow and 12 color themes, from ice blue to amber, green P1 and hot pink. Runs smoothly on older phones with three graphics levels, and works offline.

Play in English, Türkçe, Español or Português. No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device and can be exported with a save code.
```

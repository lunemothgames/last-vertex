## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
You are the last vertex: one glowing triangle at the bottom of a retro CRT terminal, and everything above it wants through.

Last Vertex is an idle tower defense game you can play actively or let run. Your triangle aims and fires on its own; you decide how it grows.

▸ BUILD IT YOUR WAY
Pour coins into permanent upgrades: damage, fire rate, crits, multishot, ricochet, pierce, armor, thorns, regen, dodge, slow, knockback and more. A wall of thorns and armor, a crit cannon, a swarm of bouncing bullets — every build is viable.

▸ TATTOOS
Summon 28 tattoos across four rarities and fuse duplicates up to level 7. Passive tattoos shape your build; active ones add a button to the battle: freeze the field, blast every enemy, overdrive your fire rate or turn kills into gold; others shatter frozen enemies into chain reactions. An epic is guaranteed within 50 summons.

▸ ENDLESS WAVES, 30 TIERS
Fight through 30 tiers of rising danger: runners, tanks, splitters, shielded wardens, healing menders, teleporting blinkers and enraged bosses that call in reinforcements. Earn mastery stars in every tier for permanent bonuses.

▸ WEEKLY OP
Every week brings two new rule-changing mutators — horde, glass cannon, boss rush, titans and more — with gem rewards for the waves you reach.

▸ PLAY ACTIVE OR IDLE
Choose your target priority, speed up the game, time your abilities — or put the phone down. Idle income keeps earning while you're away, quests refresh through the day and achievements reward every milestone.

▸ RETRO TERMINAL LOOK
Scanlines, phosphor glow and 12 color themes, from ice blue to amber, green P1 and hot pink. Runs smoothly on older phones with three graphics levels.

No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device and can be exported with a save code.
```
